ASSETS FOLDER  (public/assets)
================================
Everything in here is served by the website at /assets/...

  images/    put photos here        ->  /assets/images/my-photo.webp
  videos/    put videos (mp4/webm)  ->  /assets/videos/factory.mp4
  brand/     logos, favicon, share image
  uploads/   files uploaded from the admin panel go here automatically

In the admin panel, open  Media library -> "Assets folder"  (or any image field -> "Assets folder" tab)
to pick files you copied in by hand.

Keep photos small (WebP/JPG, under 300 KB) and videos short (MP4 H.264, under 20 MB) so the site stays fast.
Bigger videos: upload to Google Drive and paste the public link in the Media library instead.
